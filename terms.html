4. How earnings are calculated

4.1 Verified views only

Earnings are based on the view count reported by the platform's own official API — Instagram's Insights API or the YouTube Data API. We do not accept screenshots, third-party counters, or self-reported numbers as the basis for payment. If we cannot read a video's statistics through the official API, we cannot pay for it.

4.2 Minimum view threshold

Each campaign sets a minimum number of views a video must reach to earn anything. A video below that threshold earns nothing, however much effort went into it. The threshold is shown in the campaign brief before you join.

4.3 Caps

A campaign may set a maximum payout per video. Views above that cap still count toward your visible statistics but do not increase what that particular video pays.

4.4 Figures are provisional until paid

The earnings shown in your dashboard reflect the most recent view counts we have fetched, and they move up as your videos gain views. They are an estimate, not a guaranteed amount, until a payout is processed.

Once a video is paid, it is closed permanently. Its earnings are locked at the amount paid and it stops accruing. Views that video gains after the payout do not generate further earnings. This is how we keep payout history accurate and auditable — a paid record never silently changes afterwards.
