Connect Instagram

1
Use the account you'll actually post this campaign from
Each campaign is tied to one Instagram account. Pick the account you plan to post this campaign's clips on — you can connect a different account to a different campaign later.
2
Make sure it's a Professional account
Instagram only shares view counts for Business or Creator accounts — personal accounts won't work and will be rejected.
  1. Open the Instagram app on your phone
  2. Go to your profile → tap the menu (☰) → Settings and privacy
  3. Tap Account type and tools (or "Account type" on older versions)
  4. Tap Switch to professional account and choose Creator or Business
3
Accept the ClipGrow tester invite first
This step is required — without it Instagram shows an error instead of the approval screen. Easiest on a computer:
  1. Open instagram.com and log in as this account
  2. Profile icon → Edit profile (or the settings gear)
  3. Go to Apps and websites → Tester invites
  4. Accept the ClipGrow invite
No invite showing? Ask the ClipGrow admin to send one to your handle, then check again.
4
Log into that account in this browser
Instagram will connect whichever account is logged in when you continue. If a different Instagram account is currently logged in on this device, log out of it first and log into the right one.
5
Approve every permission Instagram asks for
You'll land on an Instagram screen listing what ClipGrow can access. Leave everything checked — if any permission is unchecked, view tracking won't work and you'll need to reconnect.
