**We're a clipping agency.** Brands give us their long videos — you cut them into short, punchy clips, post them to your own Instagram or YouTube, and get paid for every 1,000 views your clips bring in. No investment. No follower count needed. All the source material is provided.

**5. Make your clip**
Cut a 20–60 second vertical video from the footage using what you learned in step 2. Add captions. Keep it clean.

A Coach reviews your video and leaves specific fixes **in the same ticket**. You revise, upload a new version, paste the new link. Repeat until it's up to standard.

**When your video passes, you become an Editor** — you get your ClipGrow login and can apply to live campaigns. No deadline. Take your time and get it right.
